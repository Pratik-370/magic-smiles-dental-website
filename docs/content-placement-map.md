## 3. Detailed Our Services (`/our-services`) Placement Map

> **Service Count & Cross-Page Audit Finding:**  
> Both the source Homepage `OUR SERVICES` grid and the source `/our-services` page contain **exactly 6 cards** in a flat section (no sub-category containers in HTML). Neither the source Homepage nor `/our-services` lists 7 services:
> - **Home Card 3** (`Dental Implants`) + **Home Card 5** (`Tooth Extractions`) are consolidated on `/our-services` into **Card 3 (`Dental Implants & Restoration`)**, whose verbatim text covers both implants/crowns/bridges/dentures and gentle tooth extractions.
> - **Our Services Card 5** adds **`Pediatric Dentistry`**, which is absent as a separate Home service card.
> - **Card 6 (`Mobile Dental Outreach Programs`)** sits inside the 6-card `Our Services` section in the source HTML, yet functionally describes a mobile community outreach **program** serving schools, community centers, and local organizations. The redesigned `/our-services` page preserves slot `06` while visually distinguishing it as a dedicated **Community Outreach Program** showcase.
